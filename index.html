Quick Pitch

BAZI BA MARG — DEATH IS NOT THE END is a brutal online combat-racing game set in a post-apocalyptic industrial world. Players race heavily armored combat vehicles through dangerous tracks while using machine guns, missiles, ramming and boost systems to fight rivals, survive, and qualify for the next stage.

Core Experience

Combat RacingVehicle CombatDestructionOnline MultiplayerEliminationSpectatorProgression

Vehicle Vision

The planned roster contains 10 distinct combat vehicles. HUNTER is the flagship heavy armored AWD combat racer built around sustained fire, RAM impact, boost and guided missiles.

IRON CANYON

A planned 2.5 km post-apocalyptic industrial combat-racing track with five sectors: Industrial Gate, Canyon Bend, Bridge Run, Refinery Tunnel and Death Basin. Multiple racing lines, hazards and combat zones make route choice part of gameplay.

Online & Spectator

The long-term architecture targets competitive online races with server-authoritative combat, matchmaking, AI opponents and spectator participation after elimination, allowing eliminated players to watch the surviving competitors.

Global Tournament

A scalable global tournament structure is planned, leading through qualification stages toward a worldwide championship. Any commercial real-money tournament implementation would require appropriate legal, payment and regional compliance review.

Production Foundation

Documented foundations cover vehicle architecture, combat and destruction, track design, racing AI, multiplayer/matchmaking, spectator systems, progression, backend architecture, UI/HUD, audio/VFX, build/QA planning and IP/version-control documentation.
